### Executive Summary & Strategic Context (Page 6 — Corrected Line 17)
The RBM Partnership enters its 2026–2030 strategic cycle focused on bringing clarity, unity, and momentum to the global malaria response. To achieve this, the Partnership established a Theory of Change for the Big Push against Malaria, defining core acceleration outcomes (Big Push KPIs) and an accompanying KPI Cascade.

While the Big Push defines overall ecosystem goals and **higher-order objectives** *(corrected from "hig her order objectives")*, the Unity 30 Strategic Framework represents the action-oriented strategy for the RBM Partnership and Secretariat. Its 20 core KPIs measure specific operational and strategic activities designed to power the Big Push strategic pillars and will be reported to the Board annually. Some of the KPIs will increase reporting cadence once data workflows are completed.

The Unity 30 strategy operates through 5 core Building Blocks:
* **Engage (U.1)**: Surface country challenges and elevate shared priorities by convening stakeholders.
* **Align (U.2)**: Generate actionable intelligence and foresight through shared data and products.
* **Enable (U.3)**: Mobilize and align financial resources and local capacity to fuel action.
* **Act (U.4)**: Deliver targeted advocacy, emergency response, and country support at key execution moments.
* **Impact (U.5)**: Measure progress, evaluate partner confidence, and track reduction in global malaria burden.

#### 1. Secretariat Performance (5 KPIs)
These KPIs measure the core operational efficiency, technical assistance, and leadership confidence of the RBM Secretariat.

* **KPI.01: % convenings with outputs**
  * **Unity Building Block:** Engage (U.1) | **Owner:** Secretariat | **Cadence:** Annual
  * **Rationale:** Ensures Partnership convenings produce concrete, trackable action plans within 30 days. *(Fixed stray `|---|` markdown artifact)*
* **KPI.02: # intelligence briefs/products issued**
  * **Unity Building Block:** Align (U.2) | **Owner:** Secretariat | **Cadence:** Annual
  * **Rationale:** Measures the proactive generation and automated dissemination of strategic foresight (quarterly bulletins, CIS alerts) to align global partners.
* **KPI.03: # of countries receiving CIS reduced by > 50%**
  * **Unity Building Block:** Enable (U.3) | **Owner:** Secretariat | **Cadence:** Annual
  * **Rationale:** Tracks country self-reliance over time by monitoring the transition away from intensive routine Country Support (CIS) as local and regional capacity expands.
* **KPI.04: $ financing mobilized/unlocked through RBM CIS**
  * **Unity Building Block:** Enable (U.3) | **Owner:** Secretariat | **Cadence:** Annual

  * **Unity Building Block:** Impact (U.5) | **Owner:** Secretariat | **Cadence:** Annual
  * **Rationale:** Evaluates governance effectiveness, partner trust, and the perceived added value of the Secretariat across the partnership.

#### 2. Partnership Synergy (3 KPIs)
These metrics assess collective action, cross-organizational alignment, and country-level leadership empowerment.

* **KPI.06: % requests resolved by multi-partner teams**
  * **Unity Building Block:** Act (U.4) | **Owner:** Secretariat | **Cadence:** Annual
  * **Rationale:** Incentivizes collaborative problem-solving across working groups and stakeholder archetypes rather than siloed interventions.
* **KPI.07: # joint campaigns/actions**
  * **Unity Building Block:** Engage (U.1) | **Owner:** Advocacy & Resource Mobilization (ARCPC) | **Cadence:** Annual

### 3.1. 2026 Performance Synthesis & Strategic Situational Analysis

#### 1. Macro Epidemiological Trajectory & Exogenous Fiscal Shocks (`Save Lives` — 4% Avg. Progress to 2030 Target)
Global progress toward the 2030 Global Technical Strategy (GTS) targets remains under severe pressure. While global malaria mortality (`KPI.19`) has achieved a **7.4% reduction** against the 2015 baseline (Amber / *At Risk* against the linear milestone toward 90%), global malaria case incidence (`KPI.20`) has regressed to **-8.5%** above the 2015 baseline (Red / *Off Track*). These epidemiological headwinds reflect real operational, biological, and fiscal pressures across endemic countries—most notably the acute **2025 US Government (USG) bilateral funding cuts and broader ODA contractions**, compounded by extreme climate events, humanitarian displacement, and emerging biological resistance—rather than reporting artifacts.

#### 2. Operational Surge vs. Financing Contraction (`Unity 30 Secretariat & Partnership` — 26% & 0% Avg. Progress to 2030 Target)
The 2026 telemetry highlights a sharp divergence between high-performing Secretariat coordination outputs and the acute strain on country financing and emergency technical assistance triggered by the 2025 bilateral funding freeze:

* **Ripple Effects of the 2025 Bilateral Funding Freeze (`U.3 Enable` & `U.4 Act`)**: Rather than transitioning away from intensive Country Implementation Support (`KPI.03`, 2030 target: 50% reduction), the number of countries requiring emergency RBM technical assistance expanded by **9% (`-9%`, Red / *Off Track*)** in 2026 as National Malaria Programmes (NMPs) sought urgent support to reprogram grants, mitigate stockouts, and bridge bilateral financing cliffs. Simultaneously, overall financing unlocked via CIS contracted from **$438M in 2025 to $194M in 2026** (`KPI.04`, Red / *Off Track* vs. $5,000M cumulative 2030 target), and the surge in urgent country requests outpaced multi-partner team deployment capacity, which fell from **36% to 27%** (`KPI.06`, Red / *Off Track*).

#### 3. Ecosystem Resilience & Broader Health Integration (`Ecosystem` — 20% Avg. Progress; `Broader Goals` — 27% Avg. Progress)
Despite fiscal contraction, endemic countries and partners advanced critical structural reforms to insulate the malaria response over the medium term:

* **Market Shaping, Domestic Financing & Biological Threats (At Risk / Amber)**: Domestic co-financing ≥40% advanced from **55% to 61%** (`KPI.11`), malaria vaccine introductions expanded from **24 to 25–26 countries** (`KPI.13`), adoption of multiple first-line therapies (MFT) rose from **7% to 11%** (`KPI.12`), Dual-AI/PBO ITN reference price reductions reached **13.4%** (`KPI.15`), and refugee/IDP inclusion in Complex Operating Environment NSPs edged up from **39.1% to 41.3%** (`KPI.18`). #### 4. Methodological Rigor, Data Limitations & Forward Governance
As an inaugural operational baseline, several indicators reflect deliberate first-year methodological boundaries—including 2026 baseline establishment for African ACT manufacturing (`KPI.14` at **22%**) and Census-derived proxy baselines for partner and NMP confidence (`KPI.05` at **70%**; `KPI.08` at **80%**), pending quantification of joint advocacy campaigns (`KPI.07`), and the absence of a formalized 2030 numerical target for health-sector integration (`KPI.10`, holding at **35 countries**). These measurement limitations are transparently flagged to guide M&E refinement and are never used to excuse or mask programmatic bottlenecks, which stem from tangible financing shortfalls and elevated country implementation demand. Moving into the 2027 reporting cycle, both data quality and the frequency of reporting will be substantially increased and supported by automated data workflows across the RBM digital ecosystem. Furthermore, the reporting framework will systematically integrate additional operational context from unforeseen external shocks—such as bilateral funding contractions, supply-chain disruptions, or climate emergencies—that might endanger the achievement of our 2030 targets.
